# Silong — logo design brief

**Name:** silong (always lowercase in the wordmark). Tagalog: shelter, refuge, especially from rain.
**What it is:** a Filipino literary publishing platform for poetry, stories, personal reflections and everything left unsaid.
**Promise:** "In a world that never slows down, find your pause in writing."
**Audience:** readers and writers, mostly Filipino and mostly online, who want a quiet place to write, not a social feed.
**Adjectives:** quiet · sheltering · literary · nocturnal · timeless.
**Balance:** 85–90 % modern literary minimalism, 10–15 % pixel trace. Not a gaming brand.
**Palette:** Midnight #151D25 · Rain #263640 · Mist #506D76 · Neon #8CC3C7 · Paper #D6D5CE, plus an optional warm amber accent (proposed: Lamp #E3A95C).
**Must work at:** favicon 16/32 px, site navigation (~24–32 px tall), social avatar (circle crop), dark and light, one colour.

## Category clichés to avoid
Quill pens, open books, ink drops that look like fountain-pen ink, speech bubbles, typewriters, notebook spirals, chunky 8-bit type, glowing neon.

## Word map: the strongest intersections
- shelter × rain → **a roof**, the space underneath it
- rain × pixel → **a drop made of squares**, rain as a pixelated sign
- refuge × night × writing → **a lit window**, someone awake writing

## Assumptions
- Wordmarks are drawn from SIL Open Font License typefaces (logo use is allowed). The outlines are converted to paths, with custom pixel details added on top.
  - A: Newsreader (Production Type, OFL), a typeface made for long-form reading.
  - B: Fraunces (Undercase Type, OFL), a soft "Old Style" display serif.
  - C: Instrument Sans (OFL), a contemporary humanist-grotesque sans.
- Trademark clearance is not covered here. Do a professional search before you register anything.
