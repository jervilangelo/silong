silong · brand kit · v1.0

In a world that never slows down, find your pause in writing.

The dot of the i is a raindrop. Its left side is a smooth curve, like a pen line. Its right side steps down in three pixels, like a screen. Silong is shelter from the rain, so one drop lands inside the name.

01 · Primary wordmark

One wordmark, two colourways

Use the full-colour wordmark wherever you can. The drop takes the one accent: Neon on dark, Mist on light. Everything else is Paper or Midnight.

02 · Symbol

The drop, on its own

Use the drop where the name is already present or there's no room for it: favicons, app icons, avatars, loaders. Below 48 px, use the small-size cut. It has two larger steps so the pixels survive at tiny sizes.

04 · Space and size

Give it room to breathe

Clear space

Keep empty space equal to the height of the i (x-height) on every side. Around the symbol on its own, keep half the drop's width.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 72 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print. Symbol: 16 px using symbol-small.

In the navigation

Set the wordmark 24–32 px tall, Paper on Midnight. Don't animate it there.

05 · Don't

Keep it quiet

Don't swap the drop for a round dot
Don't recolour the letters or make the drop amber
No glow, gradients or shadows
silong.
Don't retype it or add a full stop

06 · Motion

One drop, falling once

  1. The logo rests, complete, for about 7 s. Every loop starts here.
  2. The drop fades out in place, then falls back in as the dot of the i (under 1 s).
  3. It lands with a slight squash, and the loop starts again.
  4. When the viewer prefers reduced motion, the static logo shows.

Use it on splash screens, loading states and the "publishing…" moment, not in the persistent navigation. Files: silong-wordmark-animated-on-dark.svg, -on-light.svg.
