# Silong master blueprint

Writing is the core product; atmosphere surrounds it. English-first, Filipino-centered, accessible and responsive.

Phase 1 establishes account access, public writer identities and a protected writing-room shell. No fake authentication, writing statistics or publications.

Future platform: rich-text publishing with revision history, autosaved drafts, sanitized output, reading-time estimates and accessible article pages; a private journal with private-by-default RLS; customizable rainy city, bedroom, station and cafe environments; an opt-in ambient sound mixer with independent volume controls and no autoplay; discovery through genres, search and curated collections; hearts and bookmarks with abuse controls; optional achievements without pressuring writers; report/block flows, moderation queues and appeals; an audited admin console with least-privilege roles.

Production readiness requires validated migrations, two-user authorization tests, backups and restore drills, abuse/rate controls, email delivery, privacy and terms review, observability, accessibility audit, storage validation, data export/deletion, and performance budgets. No paid resources were provisioned.
